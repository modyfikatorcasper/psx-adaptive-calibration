# PS1 / PS2 adaptive calibration research

[Full Polish research report](docs/ADAPTIVE_CALIBRATION_PL.md) · [Evidence](docs/research/center-2026-10-11/evidence.json) · [Raw data](data/README.md) · [Firmware](firmware/README.md) · [Wiring](docs/DIY_PL.md).

Measurements by Kacper Lewandowski, also known as modyfikator Modi / modyfikator 89. The public tester only reads RAW, axes, buttons and pressure. The developer interface remains local. Rumble was not tested and motor power is not connected. Results support movement-history-dependent adaptation in the tested specimens, not an exact reconstruction of Sony firmware. The modified PS1 had less repeatable centers than the tested PS2 after replacement, under different conditions.
